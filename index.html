    Campus life

    Short answers to what freshers ask most.

    Hostel

    • Carry original documents plus 4-6 photocopies and passport photos.
    • Bring a lock, extension board, bedsheets, bucket, and a basic medicine kit.
    • Learn your hostel's gate timings and visitor rules on day one.
    • Report room problems to the warden or hostel office in writing.

    Food

    • Your hostel mess is the cheapest option; check the weekly menu.
    • Food courts and shops around the central market cover snacks and late meals.
    • Carry a refillable water bottle and use filtered water points.
    • Nearby places off campus are an option for weekends.

    Classes and attendance

    • Check your timetable, section, and faculty in UMS before the first class.
    • Attendance rules are strict. Track your percentage every week.
    • Apply for leave through the official process, not by messaging a friend.
    • Keep a notebook for each course and save your login details safely.

    Clubs and activities

    • Look out for orientation week stalls and society recruitment notices.
    • Join one technical and one non-technical club to meet seniors.
    • Sports facilities are open to students; ask the sports office for timings.
    • Do not let activities hurt your attendance.

    Money and banking

    • Open or activate a bank account and keep a debit card handy.
    • Pay fees only through official portals and keep every receipt.
    • Keep a small cash reserve; UPI works at most shops nearby.
    • Never share OTPs, even with someone claiming to be staff.

    Safety and wellbeing

    • Save campus security and your warden's number in your phone.
    • Avoid unknown paying-guest or "agent" offers; use the university for housing.
    • Report ragging immediately. It is illegal and the university has an anti-ragging process.
    • Counselling is available if you feel overwhelmed. Using it is normal.

    Getting to LPU

    The campus is on the Jalandhar–Delhi highway (GT Road) near Phagwara.

    FromBest wayNotes
    PhagwaraAuto or local busClosest town, a short ride to the main gate.
    JalandharBus on GT Road, or train then autoFrequent buses; confirm the stop name with the conductor.
    LudhianaBus or train towards PhagwaraDirect buses run along the highway; trains stop at Phagwara Junction.
    Amritsar / Chandigarh / DelhiTrain to Phagwara or Jalandhar, or highway busBook trains early at the start of the session.
    AirportsAmritsar or Chandigarh, then train/busPlan extra time for the road leg.

    Always check current timings and fares on the railway and bus apps before you travel.
